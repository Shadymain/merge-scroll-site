Support

Something not working, or an idea? Email asaoluayomide5@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and what happened.

Adding comics

Tap + in the Library and choose PDFs or a whole folder in Files. Or, in another app, tap Share and choose Merge Scroll. Not sure yet? On an empty library, tap Try a sample comic.

Chapters went into the wrong series

Open the series, tap ••• and choose Merge into another series, or long-press a chapter and choose Move to series. Both can be undone, and Merge Scroll remembers the names for next time.

Hands-free reading

In the reader, tap Auto-scroll to start. Touch the page to pause. Hold the button to change the speed.

Locking a series

Open the series, tap the pencil and turn on Lock with Face ID. Locked series re-lock whenever you leave the app.

Freeing space

Settings shows what each series uses. Tap a series to remove the chapters you've read.

Your privacy

Merge Scroll works offline and collects nothing. Read the privacy policy.
